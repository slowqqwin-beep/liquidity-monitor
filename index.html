I.

System Plumbing Liquidity

The pipes. Whether short-term funding markets are clearing — repo, reserves, cross-border dollar funding. This layer is regime-agnostic: tail risk lives here, and a blockage here breaks every other thesis.

Money market spreads

SOFR–IORB and EFFR–IORB. Sustained positive drift = banks lending into repo, reserves becoming scarce. Spikes = stress events (SVB Mar-23, year-end pressure).

Reserve balances · ON RRP · Treasury General Account

Stock view of liquidity in the system. RRP drain has historically funded duration absorption; TGA rebuild pulls liquidity out.

Overnight rates layered

Where SOFR sits in the corridor — between IORB ceiling and ON RRP floor. Excursions outside the corridor = pressure.

II.

Risk Appetite Liquidity

Whether risk assets can fund themselves. Credit spreads and implied vol — this is where beta-trade conditions live. MOVE typically moves before HY spread; HY widening is the predictive signal for equity drawdowns.

Credit OAS — IG · HY · EM Corp

The hierarchy of risk premia. HY widening from current floor levels is the first signal that the AI-capex / hyperscaler funding cycle is hitting credit constraints.

Volatility regime — MOVE · VIX

MOVE leads HY by weeks. Persistent MOVE above 120 historically precedes credit stress; MOVE/VIX divergence reveals which market sees risk first.

HY / IG spread ratio

Risk-tier divergence. Rising ratio = junk underperforming investment grade = credit-cycle late-stage.

III.

Framework Liquidity

Whether the sovereign-pricing framework itself is being repriced. Gold, term premium, stablecoin growth — signals invisible in traditional rate-credit dashboards but central to the post-2022 regime where high yields can coexist with rising risk assets.

Gold / 10Y yield ratioFramework switch indicator

The cleanest single signal of regime change. Inverse pre-2022, co-moving since. A rising ratio = market discounting sovereign claims.

10Y nominal · TIPS real · Term premium decomposition

The yield's components. Nominal up + breakevens flat + term premium up = pure fiscal/credit risk repricing, not inflation or growth.

5Y5Y forward inflation expectation

The cleanest long-term inflation signal — strips out near-term noise. Anchoring above 2.5% would mean the Fed has lost the long end.

Stablecoin aggregate market capNon-sovereign dollar gauge

USDT + USDC + DAI + FDUSD. Real-time measure of dollars choosing to exit the banking system. Growth rate maps to "framework flight" intensity.

Bitcoin · Gold (rebased)

Two non-sovereign monetary assets. Co-movement = framework repricing; divergence = idiosyncratic flow.

30Y Mortgage – 10Y spread

Real-economy credit transmission. Wide spreads = MBS investors demanding compensation for prepayment + duration risk = housing market under pressure.

Reading the matrix

How to combine the three layers into a single regime read.

Layer 1 healthy + Layer 2 tight + Layer 3 framework switch

Current regime since late 2023. Plumbing fine, risk appetite high, but sovereign repricing underway. Consistent with risk-on in real assets / equities while gold grinds higher. Sustainable until L1 or L2 breaks.

Layer 1 healthy + Layer 2 widens + Layer 3 framework switch

The inflection. HY spread breaks above 4% from a sub-3% floor; MOVE persistent above 120. This is where AI/hyperscaler capex thesis meets its credit constraint. 6-12 month signal for risk drawdown.

Layer 1 stress + anything else

Pipe blockage. SOFR–IORB persistently positive, RRP drained, TGA volatile, cross-currency basis widens. All bets off — every framework loses to liquidity flight. 2008, 2020, 2023 March all started here.
